## English (U.S.)

**Name:** Squad Speak

**Subtitle:** Voice and chat for your channels

**Promotional text:** Host direct voice and Markdown chat without a general chat account.

**Keywords:** voice,chat,local network,private,channels,audio

**Description:**

SquadSpeak brings direct voice, Markdown chat and animated portraits into a compact desktop app for the channels and computers you choose. Host a channel, invite the people you trust and keep voice, messages and audio controls together.

SquadSpeak is designed around direct channels instead of a general chat account. It keeps the conversation close to the people and computers you selected.
