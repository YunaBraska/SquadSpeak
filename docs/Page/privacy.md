<header class="page-heading">
  <p class="eyebrow">Privacy</p>
  <h1>Your conversation is not a SquadSpeak account.</h1>
  <p>SquadSpeak has no central chat account, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or YunaBraska-hosted conversation service.</p>
</header>

<section class="section">
  <h2>What SquadSpeak keeps</h2>
  <p>SquadSpeak stores its local identity, channel settings, contacts and audio preferences on the computer that runs it. Microphone audio, chat messages and channel membership are handled only for the channels you join or host.</p>
  <p>Other people in a channel can receive the content you choose to send. Choose channel members and hosts carefully.</p>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Network connections</h2>
  <p>SquadSpeak connects directly to the peers and hosts you choose. YunaBraska does not operate a service that receives or stores conversation content. The direct-download edition checks GitHub for updates. GitHub receives the normal connection metadata, such as your IP address, needed to serve that request. The App Store edition uses Apple's update service instead.</p>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Your choices</h2>
  <p>Leave channels, remove local data or uninstall the app at any time. For help, use <a href="{{ site.support }}">SquadSpeak support</a>. Do not post private conversation content, invite details or connection data in a public issue.</p>
  <p>Last updated: 29 October 2026.</p>
</section>
