<section class="section">
  <h2>Talk directly. Keep the account out of it.</h2>
  <p class="section-intro">Use a local-network host name or an address you control. Each running app can host a channel. No general chat account is required.</p>
  <div class="feature-grid">
    <article class="feature"><h3>Your space, your host</h3><p>Discover nearby channels or enter a server address. Channel owners decide who can join their SquadSpeak host.</p></article>
    <article class="feature"><h3>More than voice</h3><p>Share Markdown, images and your screen. Keep private conversations alongside channels, grouped by host.</p></article>
    <article class="feature"><h3>Familiar servers</h3><p>Join TeamSpeak 3 and Mumble channels in the same interface. Provider symbols keep external connections recognisable.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>
